Small Renovation Items Worth Buying on Amazon

3 min read · Updated 2026

Not everything for a renovation needs to come from a showroom. Cabinet knobs, light fixtures, shelf brackets, drawer organizers, and similar smaller items are often available on Amazon at prices well below what local retailers charge for the same or comparable products — and delivery is usually faster than special-ordering through a contractor.

What tends to be worth it

  • Cabinet hardware — knobs and pulls are one of the easiest wins. Standard sizing means a huge range of finishes and styles are readily available, often in bulk packs that work out cheaper per piece than buying individually from a showroom.
  • Light fixtures — pendant lights, vanity bars, and flush mounts in common styles are widely available. Reviews and photos from other buyers also make it easier to judge actual size and finish before committing, which isn't always true in a dimly-lit showroom.
  • Shelving and organization — closet systems, drawer dividers, and similar organizational hardware are usually cheaper online and just as good quality as what a specialty retailer sells.
  • Small plumbing accessories — things like cabinet-mount soap dispensers, towel bars, and robe hooks are low-risk online purchases since they're simple to install and easy to return if the finish doesn't match.
Worth checking: for anything electrical, confirm the listing shows CSA or UL certification before buying — this matters for both safety and for passing inspection if your project needs a permit.

What's usually better sourced locally

  • Anything requiring exact colour matching — tile, paint, and countertop material are risky to buy sight-unseen online, since screen colours rarely match reality. See a physical sample first.
  • Major appliances and plumbing fixtures — tubs, toilets, and faucets often benefit from being seen and tested in person, and local suppliers can usually match or beat online pricing once you factor in the risk of shipping damage on something heavy and breakable.
  • Anything your contractor needs to warranty — some contractors won't warranty their installation work on materials you supply yourself. Ask before you buy, especially for anything load-bearing or plumbing-related.

A few practical tips

  • When buying multiple identical hardware pieces (like cabinet knobs for a whole kitchen), order from the same listing and batch where possible — finish can vary slightly between production runs.
  • Check the return window before your project starts, not after — many renovation purchases get opened and test-fit weeks before installation.
  • For fragile items like glass pendant lights, read recent reviews specifically for shipping damage complaints.

None of this replaces your local supplier for the bigger-ticket, harder-to-judge-online items — see our local directory for vetted tile, cabinetry, and lighting showrooms in the area. But for the smaller finishing touches, Amazon is a legitimate and often cheaper option worth checking before you default to a showroom.
