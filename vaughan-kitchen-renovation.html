What it typically costs

Kitchen remodels typically fall into three tiers: a cosmetic refresh runs roughly $10,000–$25,000, a mid-range remodel with new cabinets and layout tweaks runs $30,000–$75,000, and a full gut renovation starts around $75,000.

Local note: the City of Vaughan requires permits for plumbing relocation, electrical work beyond fixture swaps, and structural changes — factor inspection timelines into your schedule.
