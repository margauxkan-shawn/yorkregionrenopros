Local resources

York Region renovation supplier directory

A short list of real GTA-area suppliers worth knowing about while you plan — not a paid directory.

We have no paid or affiliate relationship with any business listed here. These are well-known GTA-area suppliers worth checking out — verify current hours, pricing, and availability directly with each one.

Tile & Stone

Stone Tile
Toronto showroom

Large-format tile and slab collections sourced from Europe, India, Brazil, and beyond.

Centura Tile
Multiple GTA locations

Long-established Toronto tile retailer carrying North American and European brands, including natural stone and porcelain.

Ciot
Toronto showroom

Curated tile, slab, and natural stone collection with a design-forward showroom experience.

The Tile Shoppe
Concord / Vaughan, Mississauga, Scarborough

Multiple GTA locations with a wide in-stock selection of tile and flooring, plus a trade pricing program for contractors.

Kitchen & Cabinetry

Castle Kitchens
Markham showroom

Custom kitchen and bathroom cabinetry with an in-house design and manufacturing process.

Impressions Kitchens
Toronto showroom

Custom cabinet maker known for condo and full-home kitchen renovations, including tight-space layouts.

Leicht Toronto
Toronto, Richmond Hill & Vaughan showrooms

Premium German kitchen cabinetry with integrated Miele and Gaggenau appliances.

Lighting

Union Lighting and Furnishings
Castlefield Ave, Toronto

One of Canada's largest lighting showrooms, with fixtures spanning budget to high-end, plus furniture and home accessories.

LightForm
Toronto showroom

Design-focused lighting retailer carrying international brands like Foscarini, Moooi, and Roll & Hill.

Building Materials & Hardware

Home Depot
Multiple GTA locations

General building materials, lumber, and hardware — a reliable default for standard-spec items and contractor accounts.

Rona / Lowe's
Multiple GTA locations

Building materials and hardware with a similar general-purpose scope to Home Depot — worth price-checking against for larger material orders.
