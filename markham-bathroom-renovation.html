What it typically costs

A standard bathroom refresh (new fixtures, vanity, tile) typically runs $10,000–$20,000, while a full renovation with layout changes or a curbless shower runs $20,000–$45,000+.

Local note: the City of Markham requires permits for any plumbing relocation or electrical work beyond like-for-like fixture swaps — factor inspection timelines into your schedule.

What Markham homes are actually like

Markham ranges from mature homes in Unionville and Milliken Mills to newer builds in Cornell. A lot of the renovation demand here is basement finishing, since many Markham subdivisions were originally built with unfinished lower levels.

How long does a bathroom renovation usually take?

A standard refresh runs 1–2 weeks; a full renovation with layout changes typically takes 3–5 weeks, depending on material lead times.
