What it typically costs

Basement finishing typically ranges from $25,000 for a straightforward rec-room build-out to $60,000+ for a self-contained legal suite with a separate entrance, bathroom, and kitchenette.

Local note: the City of Vaughan requires permits for a separate entrance, proper egress windows, and fire-separation compliance if you're finishing it as a legal second suite — factor inspection timelines into your schedule.

What Vaughan homes are actually like

Vaughan has a younger housing stock overall, with larger new-build homes concentrated around Kleinburg and Maple. Renovation work here tends to skew toward higher-end finishes and open-concept layout changes rather than basic code upgrades.

Can I finish my basement without a permit?

Technically no — even basic finishing work (electrical, framing) usually requires a permit, and skipping it can create problems later at resale or with an insurance claim.
